**Status: RESEARCH DRAFT — NOT HUMAN-VERIFIED, NOT APPROVED FOR NARRATION OR PUBLICATION**  
Working title: *How Humanoid Robots Are Learning to Work in Factories | Current Abilities, Limits & Future*  
Audience: US English-speaking viewers interested in AI and future technology  
Target: 8–12 minutes; evidence-led, non-hype editorial angle

## Editorial thesis

Humanoid robots have moved beyond lab-only demonstrations into selected real industrial and logistics deployments. The evidence supports task-specific, supervised/structured deployments—not a blanket claim that general-purpose humanoids can autonomously do any factory job or replace whole workforces. Distinguish company-reported metrics from independent verification, pilots from scaled rollouts, and announced future plans from completed deployments.

## Research questions that must be answered before the script can be approved

1. What tasks are shown in documented deployments, and what remains outside those task boundaries?
2. What evidence exists for intervention frequency, uptime, cycle time, error recovery, safety incidents, and total operating cost? Do not infer missing numbers.
3. Which statements are vendor/customer claims, and which have independent corroboration?
4. What worker consultation, training, safety, and job-design information is available from credible sources?
5. What is the current status of each pilot/rollout as of the final fact-check date?
