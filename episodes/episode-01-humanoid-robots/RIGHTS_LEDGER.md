## Mandatory rules

- Do not download or reuse photos, video, logos, music, or graphics from BMW, Figure, Agility, or GXO source pages unless separate reuse permission/license is documented for the exact asset and intended use.
- Prefer original visuals and diagrams. If generated imagery is used, do not present it as documentary footage; label or style it clearly as illustrative where a viewer could confuse it with real footage.
- Preserve source URL, creator, retrieval date, exact license text or permission evidence, attribution, and any restrictions for every used asset.
- No asset may be marked CLEARED until its exact file is known and its rights evidence has been reviewed.
- Unclear assets must be replaced, not presumed free.
